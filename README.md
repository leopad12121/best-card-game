Ember Court — a turn-based duel of card mages. You (the Ember Acolyte) face the Warden of the Court across a green felt table, both at 30 life. Each round your mana refills to the round number (up to 10) and you draw a card. Cards come from four original houses — Ember (damage), Tide (shield), Thorn (heal), Gale (draw) — each with a readable cost, a big value, a hand-drawn sigil and its own name. Click a card (or press 1–7) to play it, Space or the End Turn button ends your turn, and the Warden plays greedily. Reduce the Warden to 0 life to win; an empty deck deals 2 fatigue damage per draw. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best card game” bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, keyboard (1–7 to play a card, Space to end your turn, P to pause) and mouse (click a card to play it, click End Turn).
